Water Chemistry (in {{ horizon_days|default:2 }} days)
What’s this?

What

A quick snapshot of expected water chemistry at the end of your selected window.

How to read

  • pH – acidity/alkalinity (7 ≈ neutral).
  • EC (µS/cm) – salinity/ions; higher = saltier water.
  • DO (mg/L) – dissolved oxygen; higher is usually better for aquatic life.
  • Redox (mV) – oxidation-reduction potential; more positive = more oxidizing.

Why it matters

These values explain why the safety score falls into Poor / Fair / Good and guide simple checks at home or on site.
